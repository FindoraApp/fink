Not getting notifications?

Check that notifications are allowed for FINK in your iPhone's Settings → Notifications → FINK, that no Focus mode is on, and that the Ping's notifications are switched on in the app. You can send yourself a test from Settings → Send test notification in FINK.

Deleting your account

In FINK, go to Settings → Account → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account and all your Pings and matches. If you can't access the app, email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.
