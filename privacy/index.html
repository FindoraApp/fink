Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

FINK ("FINK: Find it first") watches eBay for items you describe and tells you when a match appears. This policy explains what personal data FINK collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to delete it.

Who we are

FINK is operated by Patryk, trading as 314 Labs, based in the United Kingdom. We are the data controller for the personal data described here under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Contact: threefourteenlabs@gmail.com — for any privacy question or request.

How long we keep it, and deleting your account

We keep your account, Pings, matches and device details for as long as your account exists. When you delete a single Ping, it stops being checked and disappears from the app; its data is kept until you delete your account. Server logs are kept by our hosting provider for a limited period and then deleted automatically.

To delete your account: in FINK, go to Settings → Account → Delete my account and confirm. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, sign-in, all your Pings (including ones you deleted earlier), their matches, your devices and your notification history. It cannot be undone.

If you can no longer access the app, email threefourteenlabs@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to withdraw consent at any time. Email threefourteenlabs@gmail.com and we will reply within one month. You can turn notifications off at any time in FINK or in your iPhone's Settings.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

FINK is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
